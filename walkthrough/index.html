30 days to benefits

Eight messages, from the first announcement to the morning PCMP is active.

Day 30 · Message 1 of 8

PCMP Announcement

Purpose:Introduce PCMP to all employees and highlight key benefits.

  • Target: All employees
  • Method: Company-wide email and an optional flyer
Sample email

From[Company Name] Human Resources

ToAll employees

SubjectPCMP is coming on [Effective Date]

Hello,

We are introducing PCMP. For employees Target: are eligible, participation is scheduled to begin on [Effective Date].

This note is an overview for everyone. It does not confirm your personal eligibility. Employees Target: qualify will receive their own notice.

Over the next 30 days you may receive a personal eligibility notice, an invitation to an information session, steps to download the Money$ense app, a reminder to complete online orientation, and the official Summary Plan Description.

You do not need to take action today. Questions can go to [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email].

[Company Name] Human Resources

Day 30 · Message 2 of 8

Eligibility Notices

Send individual notices of eligibility and an initial Summary Plan Description.

  • Target: Eligible employees only
  • Method: Personal email, and paper when the plan requires it
Sample email

From[Company Name] Human Resources

To[First Name]

SubjectYour PCMP eligibility notice

Hello [First Name],

This notice is just for you. Based on the plan rules, you are eligible for PCMP. Participation is scheduled to begin on [Effective Date].

An initial Summary Plan Description is included so you can start reading how the plan works. Please use that document, not this email, for plan details.

Contact [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email] if any of your information looks incorrect.

[Company Name] Human Resources

Why this day. Same day as the announcement, but private. Only people Target: qualify receive it.
