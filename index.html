PCMP 30-Day Communications Timeline

A step-by-step plan to inform, educate, and engage employees before benefits activation.

Select a step to read the sample message and see the flyer or notice that goes with it. Eight communications, from the first announcement through the day benefits begin.

  1. Who receives it
    All employees
    How it is sent
    Company-wide email, with an optional flyer or intranet post
    What you provide
    Company name, effective date, and the HR contact employees should use

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    ToAll employees

    SubjectPCMP is coming on [Effective Date]

    Hello,

    We are introducing PCMP. For employees who are eligible, participation is scheduled to begin on [Effective Date].

    This note is an overview for everyone. It does not confirm your personal eligibility. Employees who qualify will receive their own notice.

    Over the next 30 days you may receive:

    • A personal eligibility notice, if you qualify, with an initial Summary Plan Description
    • An invitation to a live information session, plus a replay if you cannot attend
    • Steps to download the Money$ense app and join the PCMP wellness group
    • A reminder to complete the PCMP online orientation
    • The official Summary Plan Description to review before the effective date

    You do not need to take action today. Please watch for the messages that apply to you.

    Questions can go to [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email].

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Sample flyer

    All employees

    PCMP is on the way

    Benefits begin [Effective Date] for eligible employees.

    • Learn what PCMP is
    • Watch for your eligibility notice
    • Get ready before day one

    A short series of messages will follow over the next 30 days. No action is needed today.

    Why this day. Everyone hears about PCMP at the same time, before personal notices go out, so the rollout does not feel sudden.

  2. Who receives it
    Eligible employees only, one person at a time
    How it is sent
    Personal email. Paper mail as well when the plan requires it
    What you provide
    The eligible-employee list and the initial SPD from your plan administrator

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectYour PCMP eligibility notice

    Hello [First Name],

    This notice is just for you. Based on the plan rules, you are eligible for PCMP.

    Your participation is scheduled to begin on [Effective Date]. An initial Summary Plan Description is included with this notice so you can start reading how the plan works.

    Please:

    • Read this notice and the enclosed Summary Plan Description
    • Watch for a separate invitation to an information session
    • Contact [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email] if any of your information looks incorrect

    This message was sent only to employees who meet the eligibility rules. The Summary Plan Description, not this email, is the source for plan details.

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Sample notice

    Individual notice

    Notice of eligibility

    Employee[First Name]
    PlanPCMP
    StatusEligible
    Effective[Effective Date]

    Sent only to employees who qualify. The SPD is supplied by the plan administrator.

    Why this day. Same day as the general announcement, but private. Only people who qualify receive this notice and the initial SPD.

  3. Who receives it
    All employees, so people who are unsure can attend and ask
    How it is sent
    Email plus a calendar invitation. The replay link is in the same message
    What you provide
    Session dates, the meeting link, and who will host

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    ToAll employees

    SubjectYou are invited: PCMP information session

    Hello,

    Join a live session to see how PCMP works and to ask questions before [Effective Date].

    Live session
    [Session Date]
    [Session Time]
    Join: [Session Link]

    Cannot attend live? A replay will be available at [Replay Link] within one business day.

    We will cover:

    • What PCMP is and how eligibility works
    • What employees are asked to do before [Effective Date]
    • How to download the Money$ense app and join the PCMP wellness group
    • Where to read the Summary Plan Description

    Come with questions. Plan details in the session follow the Summary Plan Description.

    [HR Contact Name]
    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Sample flyer

    Live or replay

    PCMP information session

    When[Session Date]
    Time[Session Time]
    Join[Session Link]
    Replay[Replay Link]

    Bring questions. If you cannot attend live, the replay is in the same invitation.

    Why this day. Four days after the announcement, employees can hear it explained and ask questions, with a replay for every shift.

  4. Who receives it
    Eligible employees
    How it is sent
    Email, after the information session so the ask is expected
    What you provide
    The wellness group name and join code employees should use

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectDownload Money$ense and join the PCMP wellness group

    Hello [First Name],

    Please download the Money$ense app and join the PCMP wellness group before [Effective Date]. Doing this now means you are set up before benefits begin.

    1. Download the Money$ense app.
    2. Create your account with your work email.
    3. Join the [Company Name] PCMP wellness group with code [Group Code].

    The group is for eligible employees. Session replays, orientation reminders, and updates are posted there.

    Need help getting in? Contact [HR Email].

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Sample card

    Money$ense

    Join the PCMP wellness group

    For eligible employees at [Company Name].

    1. Download the Money$ense app
    2. Sign up with your work email
    3. Enter group code [Group Code]

    Why this day. The app ask comes after employees have heard what PCMP is, and before orientation, so the group is ready when the course opens.

  5. Who receives it
    Eligible employees
    How it is sent
    Email with a direct link to the course
    What you provide
    The course link and about how long it takes. People who already finished can ignore it

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectPlease complete your PCMP orientation

    Hello [First Name],

    Please complete the PCMP online orientation before [Effective Date]. It takes about [Course Length] and explains the benefit in plain language.

    Start here: [Orientation Link]

    The course walks through:

    • How PCMP works for eligible employees
    • What to expect on [Effective Date]
    • How the Money$ense app and wellness group are used
    • Who to contact with questions

    When you finish, you do not need to send anything back. If you have already completed the orientation, thank you. You can ignore this note.

    [HR Contact Name]
    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Why this day. Two weeks out, the course is the main thing left to do besides reading the plan document.

  6. Who receives it
    Eligible employees
    How it is sent
    Personal email with the official SPD attached or linked
    What you provide
    The official SPD from your plan administrator, and where employees should open it

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectPlease review your PCMP Summary Plan Description

    Hello [First Name],

    Please review your Summary Plan Description for PCMP before benefits begin on [Effective Date].

    The Summary Plan Description is the official description of the plan. Please use that document, not this email, for plan details.

    Open your SPD: [SPD Link]

    If something is unclear, contact [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email], or use the contact listed inside the SPD.

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Cover sheet only

    Official plan document

    Summary Plan Description

    PlanPCMP
    For[Company Name]
    Review by[Effective Date]

    This page shows the cover note employees receive. The SPD itself is supplied separately and is not reproduced here.

    Why this day. Eligible employees already received an initial SPD with their eligibility notice. This follow-up puts the official document in front of them again, with ten days left to ask questions.

  7. Who receives it
    Eligible employees
    How it is sent
    Email. An optional note gives managers a 30-second script
    What you provide
    Confirmation of the effective date. The manager note is optional

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectOne week until PCMP begins

    Hello [First Name],

    PCMP becomes active on [Effective Date], one week from today. Here is a short checklist:

    • Confirm you received your eligibility notice
    • Read the Summary Plan Description: [SPD Link]
    • Finish the online orientation: [Orientation Link]
    • Download Money$ense and join the PCMP wellness group with code [Group Code]

    If you have already done these, you are set. Nothing else is required before the effective date.

    Questions: [HR Contact Name], [HR Email].

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Optional manager note

    You do not need to explain the plan. If it comes up this week, this is enough:

    PCMP starts on [Effective Date]. Employees who are eligible have already received their notice. The reminders are to read the Summary Plan Description, finish the online orientation, and download the Money$ense app to join the PCMP wellness group.

    Questions go to [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email].

    Why this day. One checklist, not a new topic. People who are finished can ignore it. Managers are asked not to interpret eligibility or plan terms.

  8. Who receives it
    Eligible employees
    How it is sent
    Email the morning of the effective date
    What you provide
    A send time on the morning benefits begin

    Sample message

    From[Company Name] Human Resources

    To[First Name]

    SubjectPCMP is active today

    Hello [First Name],

    PCMP is now active for you as of today, [Effective Date].

    You can:

    • Use the benefit described in your Summary Plan Description
    • Open the Money$ense app and visit the PCMP wellness group
    • Revisit the orientation any time: [Orientation Link]
    • Contact [HR Contact Name] at [HR Email] with questions

    Thank you for taking the time to get ready.

    [Company Name] Human Resources

    Sample card

    Effective today

    PCMP is active

    For eligible employees of [Company Name].

    Effective[Effective Date]
    • Read the benefit in your SPD
    • Open the Money$ense wellness group
    • Questions go to HR

    Why this day. Eligible employees get a clear confirmation that the benefit has started, and where to go if they still have a question.

Sample messages are a starting point. Bracketed fields are filled in for each employer. Eligibility, plan terms, and the Summary Plan Description come from the employer and the plan administrator. This page is a preview for discussion. It is not a plan document, an offer of benefits, or a determination of who is eligible.
